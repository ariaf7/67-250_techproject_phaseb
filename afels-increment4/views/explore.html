Mission Statement

MonoMuse is dedicated to exploring the evolving relationship between art, technology, and society. Our mission is to inspire curiosity and creativity by showcasing innovative exhibitions that highlight how digital tools, emerging technologies, and human imagination shape the future of artistic expression.

Who We Serve

MonoMuse is dedicated to serving everyone, from scholars and children, and anyone in between. MonoMuse prides itself on being accessible to everyone, so that no matter your background, something can be learned from visiting.

Founding Year

MonoMuse has been around since 2005 (October 7th, specifically), a museum founded when Arianne Fels, the daughter of two philanthropists was born.

Milestones

  • 2023: Expansion of the museum space and launch of international artist collaborations.
  • 2018: Rebuilt to be the beautiful castle built in the honor of Arianne that is known all around the world.
  • 2005: MonoMuse opens, with baby Arianne cutting the ribbon.
